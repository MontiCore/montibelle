CyberBelle

Based on a well-established mathematical dataflow-theory, the CyberBelle SysMLv2Prover applies logic, knowledge representation and intelligent reasoning to software engineering to perform correctness proofs, execute symbolic tests or find counterexamples.
At its core, the mature theorem prover Isabelle serves as the reasoning engine. Isabelle allows system knowledge to be represented as a collection of mathematical definitions and theorems, over which intelligent logical reasoning can be performed.

Before exploring larger, more complex systems, we introduce a minimal model to illustrate the fundamental capabilities of our analysis tool. The SysML v2 analysis tool has a connection to the theorem prover Isabelle in read-only mode.
